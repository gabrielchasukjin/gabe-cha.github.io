    ContentGen: Improving LLM-Generated Educational Content for Data Science↗

    Jiaen Yu*, Ylesia Wu*, Gabriel Cha, Ayush Shah, Sam Lau · UC San Diego

    We needed practice problems for the intro data science courses we taught at UCSD, so we built a JupyterLab tool that drafts questions from the instructor's own notebook, then tested which prompts actually made them better. Six instructors tried it and found it useful.

  • 02Open source · 2025 · ★ 395 on GitHub

    Y Combinator interview, Summer 2025

    Cloi: a local debugging agent for the terminal↗

    Most AI debuggers send your stack traces and source code to someone else's server, which is a dealbreaker when your code or data is private. I wanted one that never leaves your laptop. Cloi reads the error, pulls in the parts of your codebase that matter, and suggests a small fix you can accept or reject — because most bugs just need the right context.

  • 03Research · 2024

    Funded by the UCSD HDSI Undergraduate Research Scholarship

    Speech emotion recognition with CNNs, SVMs, and ViTs↗

    Can a model hear how someone feels? Two teammates and I trained models on 12,000 voice clips, and our best one got 86% right. What stayed with me was how sure it was when it was wrong — calling anger sadness with high confidence. That's what pulled me toward making models easier to understand.

  • 04Study project · 2023

    An N-Gram language model from first principles↗

    I wanted to understand language models from the ground up, so I built the simplest one: guess the next word by counting what usually comes next. It sounds fluent two words at a time but wanders off over a full sentence, which made me appreciate how much today's models quietly keep track of.
